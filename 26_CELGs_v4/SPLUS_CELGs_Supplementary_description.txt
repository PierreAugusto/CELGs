Survey: S-PLUS Compact Emission-Line Galaxies (CELGs)

This Supplementary Target catalogue for the 4MOST survey aims to spectroscopically observe Compact Emission-Line Galaxies (CELGs) identified from the Southern Photometric Local Universe Survey (S-PLUS). CELGs are characterized by compact morphology and strong emission lines, making them excellent laboratories for studying star formation processes, AGN activity, and galaxy evolution in the local universe. The selected targets represent a homogeneous sample of emission-line galaxies with photometric redshift estimates in the range z ~ 0.25-0.35, providing an opportunity to study the physical properties and environmental effects on these systems through spectroscopic observations with 4MOST's low-resolution spectrograph.

There are no subsurveys.

Brief science goal description: Spectroscopic characterization of compact emission-line galaxies from S-PLUS to study star formation, AGN activity, and galaxy evolution in the local universe (z ~ 0.25-0.35).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Source of target catalogue selection

Targets are selected from the Southern Photometric Local Universe Survey (S-PLUS), which provides multi-band photometry including narrow-band filters sensitive to emission lines. The parent catalogue is cross-matched with Gaia DR3 for proper astrometric information (coordinates, proper motions, and parallaxes).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 Target selection overview:

The target selection identifies 516 Compact Emission-Line Galaxies (CELGs) based on the following criteria:

* Photometric identification of emission-line galaxies using S-PLUS narrow-band filters
* Compact morphology consistent with CELGs classification
* Photometric redshift range: 0.25 < z_phot < 0.35
* Magnitude range: 19.3 < G_mag < 22.0 (Gaia G-band)
* Sky coverage: –80° < Dec < +5° (Southern hemisphere)
* Full sky RA coverage: 1.6° < RA < 359.3°

All targets are classified as galaxies (CLASSIFICATION = 'GAL') and will be observed with the Low-Resolution Spectrograph (LRS, R~5000) to obtain emission-line diagnostics including [OII], Hβ, [OIII], Hα, [NII], and [SII] lines.

The targets are matched with Gaia DR3 to:
* Ensure proper astrometric accuracy
* Exclude stellar contaminants using proper motion and parallax information
* Provide epoch 2016.0 coordinates with proper motion corrections

Target selection criteria:
1. Strong emission-line candidates from S-PLUS photometry
2. Compact galaxy morphology (CELG characteristics)
3. Cross-match with Gaia DR3 for astrometry (within 1 arcsec)
4. No significant proper motion (stellar contamination check)
5. Photometric redshift in the target range

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 Target coordinates, proper motion, and parallaxes

Coordinates: J2000 equatorial coordinates (RA, DEC) from Gaia DR3
Epoch: 2016.0
Proper motions: PMRA and PMDEC from Gaia DR3 (in mas/yr)
Parallaxes: From Gaia DR3 (in mas)

The astrometric information is used to ensure accurate fiber positioning and to verify that targets are extragalactic (negligible proper motion and parallax consistent with distant objects).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4 Target photometry

MAG: Gaia G-band magnitude (Vega system)
MAG_ERR: 0.01 mag (typical Gaia photometric uncertainty)
MAG_TYPE: Gaia_G_Vega

Calibration magnitudes:
* CAL_MAG_BLUE: Gaia BP magnitude (GAIA_GBP_VEGA_PSF)
* CAL_MAG_GREEN: Gaia G magnitude (GAIA_G_VEGA_PSF)
* CAL_MAG_RED: Gaia RP magnitude (GAIA_GRP_VEGA_PSF)
* CAL_MAG_ERR: 0.01 mag for all bands

All photometry is from Gaia DR3, ensuring homogeneous and well-calibrated measurements across the entire sample.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.5 Redshifts

REDSHIFT_ESTIMATE: Photometric redshift from S-PLUS multi-band photometry
Redshift range: 0.25 < z_phot < 0.35
REDSHIFT_ERROR: Photometric redshift uncertainty (typical σ_z ~ 0.02-0.05)

The spectroscopic observations will provide accurate spectroscopic redshifts and validate the photometric redshift estimates from S-PLUS.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.6 Reddening

REDDENING: Galactic extinction correction applied based on dust maps
The reddening corrections are relatively small for most targets as they are selected to avoid high-extinction regions.

1.9. Any additional information

Scientific motivation:
Compact Emission-Line Galaxies represent an important population for understanding galaxy evolution, as they may represent:
1. Starburst galaxies in active star formation phase
2. Green pea analogs at intermediate redshift
3. Low-mass AGN hosts
4. Systems undergoing transformation processes

The spectroscopic observations will:
* Confirm photometric redshifts
* Classify ionization mechanisms (SF vs. AGN)
* Measure gas-phase metallicities
* Determine star formation rates
* Study the physical conditions in compact star-forming systems

Sample characteristics:
* Total targets: 516
* Resolution: Low-Resolution Spectrograph (LRS, RESOLUTION=1)

Target-selection code:

The target selection was performed using S-PLUS photometric data and Gaia DR3 astrometry. The main selection steps include:

1. Selection of emission-line galaxy candidates from S-PLUS narrow-band photometry
2. Application of compact morphology criteria (CELG selection)
3. Photometric redshift cut: 0.25 < z_phot < 0.35
4. Magnitude selection: 19.3 < G_mag < 22.0
5. Cross-match with Gaia DR3 (1 arcsec radius)
6. Stellar contamination removal using proper motion and parallax
7. Quality cuts on photometric measurements
